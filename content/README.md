# Starter Content

- Words defined in `content/starter/words.json` with syllable breakdowns and media references.
- Placeholder images are simple SVGs created for this repo (license-safe, CC0-equivalent by author).
- Audio placeholders are silent text markers until real recordings are added; replace with licensed or self-recorded audio before production.

## Adding new content
1. Add word entry to `content/starter/words.json` with `imagePath` and `audioPath`.
2. Place corresponding media under `public/assets/starter/images` and `public/assets/starter/audio`.
3. Update tags/difficulty to guide lesson selection.
